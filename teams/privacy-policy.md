# Privacy notice

Ack-it-fast is an internal company tool. Its Teams calling feature places a brief call that is intended to appear as a missed call and notify the recipient.

Ack-it-fast does not record or transcribe calls, and does not store call content. Microsoft Teams and the calling service process the recipient's Teams identity and basic call signaling information to place and deliver the call; Microsoft may handle this information under your organization's Microsoft 365 agreement and policies.

For privacy questions, contact your company's IT or privacy team.
